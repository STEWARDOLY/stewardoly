This Privacy Policy describes how [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME] ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Stewardoly (大管家) online board game and related services ("the Service").

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the minimum information needed to run the game. If you have any questions, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL].

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Display name: A short name (up to 14 characters) you choose to identify yourself in a game room. This is not your real name unless you choose to use it.
  • Chat messages: Text messages you send to other players in a game room.
  • Hero selection and game choices: Your in-game decisions (which token you pick, which cards you draw, etc.) — used only to run the game.
  • Payment information (if you purchase a paid tier): Processed entirely by our payment partner Lemon Squeezy. We do not store credit card numbers, bank details, or full payment data on our servers.

Information collected automatically

  • Browser language preference: Used to display the game in your preferred language (English or Traditional Chinese). Stored in your browser's local storage, not on our servers.
  • Session data: A temporary room code and connection identifier used only while you play. Discarded when the room closes (6 hours of inactivity).
  • Technical logs: Server logs may temporarily record IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps for security and debugging. These are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Real names, addresses, or phone numbers
  • Email addresses (unless you purchase the paid version through Lemon Squeezy)
  • Location data beyond approximate country (from IP, for security only)
  • Browsing history outside our Service
  • Biometric or facial data

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the game (match players to rooms, sync game state, deliver chat messages)
  • To remember your language preference between visits
  • To process paid subscriptions (handled by Lemon Squeezy)
  • To respond to your support inquiries
  • To detect and prevent abuse, cheating, or security threats

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties. Information is shared only as follows:

  • With other players in your room: Your display name, hero selection, in-game actions, and chat messages are visible to everyone in the same game room. This is necessary for multiplayer play.
  • With Lemon Squeezy (payment processor): If you purchase a paid plan, your name, email, and payment details are processed by Lemon Squeezy under their Privacy Policy.
  • With our hosting provider (Railway): Application logs and server data are stored on Railway's infrastructure under their privacy terms.
  • When required by law: If compelled by valid legal process in Taiwan or another applicable jurisdiction.

4. Affiliate Links

The Service may contain affiliate links to third-party Christian resources (such as bookstores or Bible apps). If you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to resources we genuinely recommend. See the affiliate disclosure on each page where such links appear.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not traditional cookies) to remember:

  • Your language preference
  • Your last room code (for 6 hours, to allow quick rejoining after a disconnect)

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention

  • Game rooms: Automatically deleted 6 hours after the last activity.
  • Chat messages: Stored only during the active game session, then discarded.
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 30 days for security purposes.
  • Payment records: Retained by Lemon Squeezy per their policy and applicable tax law (typically 7 years for tax records).

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] and we will delete it promptly.

For users aged 13–18, we recommend playing with parental awareness and consent.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL].

For users in Taiwan: Your rights are protected under the Personal Data Protection Act (個人資料保護法).

For users in the EU/UK: Your rights are protected under GDPR. We rely on legitimate interest (for game operation) and consent (for payment processing) as legal bases.

For users in California, USA: The CCPA grants you rights to access, delete, and opt out of "sale" of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption for all traffic. However, no system is 100% secure. Please use a unique display name and avoid sharing personal information in game chat.

10. International Data Transfer

Our servers are hosted on Railway, which may store data in regions including the United States and the European Union. By using the Service, you consent to such transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates the latest revision.

隱私政策

本隱私政策說明 [您的姓名／公司名稱]（以下稱「我們」）如何於您使用「大管家」（Stewardoly）線上桌遊及相關服務（以下稱「本服務」）時蒐集、使用與保護資訊。

我們尊重您的隱私，僅蒐集為運作遊戲所必需的最少資訊。若有任何問題，歡迎聯絡：[您的電子郵件]

一、我們蒐集的資訊

您主動提供的資訊

  • 顯示名稱：您於遊戲房間中選擇的暱稱（最多 14 個字元）。除非您自行選擇，否則非您的真實姓名。
  • 聊天訊息：您於遊戲房間中傳送給其他玩家的文字訊息。
  • 角色選擇與遊戲決策：您於遊戲中所做的選擇（選擇的角色、抽到的卡牌等），僅用於遊戲運作。
  • 付款資訊（若購買付費版本）：由我們的金流合作夥伴 Lemon Squeezy 全程處理。我們的伺服器不會儲存信用卡號、銀行資訊或完整付款資料。

自動蒐集的資訊

  • 瀏覽器語言偏好：用於以您偏好的語言顯示遊戲（英文或繁體中文）。儲存於您的瀏覽器本地端，非我們的伺服器。
  • 連線資料：遊戲期間的暫時房間代碼與連線識別碼，房間關閉後（6 小時無活動）即刪除。
  • 技術日誌：伺服器可能暫時記錄 IP 位址、瀏覽器類型與時間戳記，用於安全與除錯目的。保留時間最長 30 天。

我們不蒐集的資訊

  • 真實姓名、地址或電話號碼
  • 電子郵件地址（除非您透過 Lemon Squeezy 購買付費版本）
  • 除 IP 推測之國家層級外的位置資料（僅用於安全目的）
  • 本服務以外的瀏覽紀錄
  • 生物特徵或臉部資料

二、如何使用您的資訊

  • 運作遊戲（媒合玩家、同步遊戲狀態、傳遞聊天訊息）
  • 記住您下次到訪的語言偏好
  • 處理付費訂閱（由 Lemon Squeezy 處理）
  • 回應您的客服查詢
  • 偵測與防範濫用、作弊或安全威脅

三、資訊分享

我們不會販售、出租或交易您的資訊予任何第三方。資訊僅於下列情況分享：

  • 與同房間的其他玩家：您的顯示名稱、角色選擇、遊戲動作與聊天訊息會被同一遊戲房間中的所有玩家看見。此為多人連線遊戲所必需。
  • 與 Lemon Squeezy（金流處理商）：若您購買付費方案，您的姓名、電子郵件與付款細節將依其隱私政策由 Lemon Squeezy 處理。
  • 與我們的雲端供應商（Railway）：應用程式日誌與伺服器資料儲存於 Railway 的基礎設施，並適用其隱私條款。
  • 依法律要求：若臺灣或其他適用司法管轄區之有效法律程序要求時。

四、聯盟連結

本服務可能包含通往第三方基督教資源（如書店或聖經應用程式）的聯盟連結。若您透過這些連結進行購買，我們可能會獲得少額佣金，您不會因此支付額外費用。我們僅推薦真心信任的資源。詳細聯盟揭露聲明，請見出現連結頁面之說明。

五、Cookie 與本地儲存

我們使用瀏覽器的本地儲存（非傳統 Cookie），用於記住：

  • 您的語言偏好
  • 您最後使用的房間代碼（保留 6 小時，便於斷線後快速重新加入）

您隨時可於瀏覽器設定中清除此資料。
